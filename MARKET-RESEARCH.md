## 4. Recommended house-call pricing

Anchors: DMV Mobile Barber at $100 with travel, StylesGo at $75–$95, and Jordy's studio Haircut & Beard at $50. A luxury mobile visit should clear roughly **2x the studio price** to cover drive time, setup, and the "one client at a time" opportunity cost.

| House-call service | Suggested | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| The House Call — Fade or Taper | **$85** | Sits under DMV Mobile Barber, above StylesGo Essential, ~2.1x studio |
| The Full Ritual — Cut + Beard + hot towel | **$110** | Signature service; matches DMV Mobile Barber's top single-client rate |
| Add a second person (same address) | **+$45** | Undercuts $35+cut combos while rewarding group bookings |
| Kids add-on (with an adult cut) | **+$35** | |
| Event / wedding party (3+) | **from $200/hr** or $75 per head | Mirrors "Presidential" tier; deposit required |
| Monthly membership (2 visits) | **$180/mo** | Locks recurring revenue; DMV Mobile Barber does $350 for 4 |
| Travel | included within ~15 mi of Annapolis; **+$1.50/mi** beyond | Keeps the "travel included" promise that every premium comp makes |

Positioning line for the site and Booksy: **"Studio pricing at the studio. House calls from $85, travel included."**

## 5. Quick wins beyond pricing

1. **Reviews.** 40 → 100 is the single biggest lever vs. the 189-review competitor. Add a "leave a review" link in Booksy's post-appointment message.
2. **Booksy listing name.** "J.Barber 1" doesn't match "Jordy Cutz" on Instagram or the site. Renaming the Booksy business to "Jordy Quiroz", the name the site and domain now use, would unify search results.
3. **Add a House Call service to Booksy** with the price above so clients can book it directly instead of leaving a note.
4. **Gift cards** are already enabled on Booksy — promote them on the site before the holidays.
5. **Bilingual advantage.** Annapolis has a large Spanish-speaking client base and few competitors advertise it. It's on the site; it should be on the Booksy description too.
