    07 — Questions

    Good to know.

    How do house calls work?

    Book your service on Booksy and add your address in the note, or DM on Instagram to arrange. Jordy arrives with a full setup — chair, cape, tools, hot towels. House calls are one flat $200 per visit, whatever you pick from the menu, with travel and setup included; group and event bookings welcome.

    What's included with the Haircut & Beard?

    A full fade or taper, beard alignment and profiling, hot-towel steam and massage, and a razor-clean finish. It's the signature service and runs about 45 minutes.

    Do you cut kids?

    Yes — regular cuts and fades for kids, 30 minutes, with plenty of patience. Designs are available for kids too.

    Can I get a custom design?

    Absolutely. Lines, arrows, logos, lettering — bring a reference or describe the idea. Design + fade runs about 55 minutes.

    ¿Hablas español?

    Sí. Jordy atiende en inglés y español. Reserva por Booksy o escribe por Instagram.

    Cancellations?

    Life happens — just reschedule through Booksy as early as you can so the slot can go to someone else. Booksy's payment and cancellation policy applies.
